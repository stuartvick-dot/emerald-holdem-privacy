Emerald Hold'em Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Emerald Hold'em is a Texas Hold'em poker game featuring virtual chips, multiplayer tables, and in-game chat. No real-money wagering or cash prizes are offered.

1. Information We Process

To provide gameplay and multiplayer features, the app may process:

  • Player display names and identifiers.
  • Profile pictures voluntarily selected by players.
  • Chat messages sent during multiplayer games.
  • Game activity, cards, bets, chip balances, and table state.
  • Connection information such as IP addresses, session identifiers, and reconnect tokens.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate multiplayer tables, identify players during games, display player profiles, deliver chat messages, synchronize gameplay, maintain connections, and protect game integrity.

3. Information Shared With Other Players

Other players at a table may see your display name, selected profile picture, chat messages, and gameplay information that the game is designed to reveal. Private cards are not intended to be disclosed to opponents except when game rules or your choices permit their disclosure.

4. Service Providers

Emerald Hold'em uses Render to host its multiplayer server. Connection and gameplay information passes through this service to operate online games. Render may also process technical connection and operational information under its own policies.

5. Advertising and Tracking

Emerald Hold'em does not currently include third-party advertising or advertising-tracking software. Player information is not intentionally shared with advertising data brokers for cross-app tracking.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Emerald Hold'em processes multiplayer gameplay, player profiles, chat messages, and session information to operate online poker games.

Game session data is stored temporarily on the multiplayer server. Empty or disconnected tables automatically expire after 30 minutes. Tables with connected players but no activity automatically expire after 24 hours.

When a table expires, its associated gameplay, player profile, chat, and session data are removed from the active game server.

Our hosting provider, Render, may separately retain operational, security, and connection records according to its own retention practices. Application logs are generally retained for seven days on our current Hobby workspace plan. Other infrastructure records may have different retention periods.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Players can choose their display name and whether to use a custom profile picture.

Players can delete their active multiplayer session information by opening Settings in Emerald Hold'em and selecting "Leave / Delete Session Data".

This action revokes the player's reconnect credentials and removes associated profile information, avatar data, chat messages, moderation reports, and session records from the active multiplayer server. Information necessary to safely complete an active poker hand may temporarily remain in anonymized form.

Hosting-provider operational and security records may be retained separately according to applicable retention policies.

Players may also request information about their privacy rights using the contact method provided below.

8. Children and Age Rating

Emerald Hold'em features simulated gambling using virtual chips and is intended for audiences permitted by its applicable App Store age rating. It is not intended for children.

9. Security

Multiplayer connections use encrypted transport (TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect game data, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
