Emerald Hold'em Support

Welcome to the official support page for Emerald Hold'em, a free Texas Hold'em poker game for iPhone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Emerald Hold'em free?

Yes. Emerald Hold'em uses virtual chips. There is no real-money wagering, cash gambling, or cash prizes.

How do I play with friends?

Open the game, create a multiplayer table, and share your table's invitation code with a friend so they can join.

What if I lose my connection?

Reconnect to your existing game session to restore your seat when the session is still available.

How do I delete my session data?

Open Settings in Emerald Hold'em and choose "Leave / Delete Session Data." This removes the associated session information from the active game server, subject to the conditions described in our privacy policy.

How do I report a problem?

If you experience an issue, please use the support contact information provided in the app or its privacy policy.
